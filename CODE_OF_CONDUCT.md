# Code of Conduct / 社群行為準則

We welcome educators, students, developers, and other contributors.

## Our standards
- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Offer constructive feedback and respect different experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or disclose private information about others.
- Do not share real student records or personally identifying information in public issues.

## Reporting and enforcement
If you encounter unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer through a private channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile, if available. Do not include sensitive information in public GitHub issues. The maintainer may remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation.

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
